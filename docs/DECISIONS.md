# Decisions and manual quirks

Things found while building the question bank that are worth knowing before changing it.

## Where the manual differs from common belief

- **No school zone speed number.** Many study guides say 15 mph. The manual (p. 47) gives no number: school zone limits apply "when children are going to or from the school or during a school recess hour", and speeding there is reckless driving with 6 points. No question uses 15 mph.
- **No pentagon or "pennant".** The manual never says a school sign is a pentagon, and calls the no-passing sign "a triangular No Passing Zone sign" (p. 37), not a pennant. Neither word is used as a fact.
- **Train stopping distance** is "between 15 and 50 feet from the railroad tracks" (p. 50). Page 113 words it as from the nearest rail.
- **Move Over law** (pp. 55, 72): change to a lane not next to the stopped vehicle; slow down only when changing lanes is impossible or unsafe. It is not "move over or slow down" as equal choices.
- **Dimming high beams** (pp. 45, 74): within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle, and also when meeting or overtaking within 500 feet.
- **Turn signal** is on p. 56 (at least 50 feet before the turn), not p. 55.

## Left out on purpose

- **Back-seat belt age.** Page 31 says back-seat passengers "under 17" must buckle up, and the next line says the rule stops at "18 years or older". The question was dropped rather than teach a number that may be wrong.
- **Facts that only appear in pictures** (flagger hand signals, lane-control symbols, route marker shields): the text extraction can't support them.
- **Statistics, fees, and licensing paperwork**: not test material.
- **The points table**: the PDF scrambles its columns, so only clearly aligned rows were used.

## Quotes from tables

Five quotes come from tables the PDF extracts out of order (a-022, a-050, a-084, a-156, a-157). They were checked by hand against the printed manual and are listed as exceptions in `tests/check_quotes.py`.

## App behavior

- **Old saved progress key.** Before accounts, the app saved progress in the browser under `tn-permit-daily-v1`. That key is now read only once, for the one-time import: the first time a Learner with no records signs in on a device that has it, the old progress becomes that Learner's Imported record. The app never writes or deletes the key. The device remembers that the import is done (under `rrtn-old-progress-imported`, which signing out leaves in place) and doesn't repeat it, so on a shared device only the first Learner to sign in there gets the import. A Helper signing in never triggers it.
- **Progress lives on the Instance.** The server stores each Learner's records, and the page works out Progress from them (`docs/adr/0001-server-stores-records-page-works-out-progress.md`). Each device keeps a copy of the signed-in Learner's records under `rrtn-learner-<account id>`, so the app opens quickly and works offline. Sets finished offline wait in that copy and are sent when the device is back online. Signing out needs a connection: it sends anything still waiting, then deletes that Learner's copy from the device.
- **Standings, not scores.** Each Fact has a Standing (new, seen, learned, solid) worked out from its counted answers, and is due again after a wrong answer at once, else 12 hours, 48 hours, then every 7 days after its first, second and third spaced success (two right answers count separately only 12 hours apart). The home screen and Set done show how many Facts are learned and solid, never a score; Sections show "x of 10 learned" behind "See each section" (`docs/adr/0002-standings-and-the-due-ladder.md`, #34, #37).
- **Half-finished Sets are saved** after each answer, in the device copy and on the Instance (every screen of the Set with its question id, the shown answer order, and answers so far, re-asks included), so a Learner can stop on one device and go on with the Set on another. Each Learner has at most one; the one saved most recently wins. A Reset or Move drops it, on the Instance and on every device, even one that was offline at the time. If the questions change so a saved Set no longer matches, it is dropped quietly. "Stop for now and save my place" goes back to the home screen, which shows the saved Set as one line with "Continue where I stopped". Starting a different Set while answers are saved asks first and says those answers will be cleared (#32).
- **"Another set"** is the main button after a Set while anything is due or new, and "I'm done for now" comes second (#31). When nothing is due and nothing is new, "I'm done for now" is the main button and "Another set" is the quiet choice, with a line saying the next Set goes over Facts already known.
- **Lessons teach; Sets check.** A Section with a lesson (`data/lessons/section-N.json`) offers it on the home screen before the Set that would first ask about its Facts, as the main button. Every lesson step asks the Learner to act, on a top-down drawing or among choices, before it states the rule; a wrong action is said plainly ("You picked: your car.") and the rule follows either way. The manual's sentences are never shown. A finished lesson is a record of type `lesson` whose answers change no Standing; from then on the Section's new Facts are asked as counted questions with no First look or Rule screen, since the lesson was their first encounter. After a wrong counted answer on a taught Fact, "See how this works" shows its lesson step and comes back. Lessons stay open under "Lessons" on the home screen. A lesson stopped partway is not saved. The format and the drawing kit are in `docs/specs/lessons.md`.
- **A Set is built, not chosen.** Each Set holds up to 10 counted questions: due Facts first (wrong ones first), then up to 5 new Facts from the Section a Move last named (Section 1 by default), and when that is fewer than 5, Facts not yet due. A new Fact starts with a First look (an unscored guess), then its Rule screen with what was picked and the answer, and its counted question comes at least 4 screens for other Facts later, never right after its rule (#33 reworked). There is no "Show me the rule again" before a counted question, so every counted answer is recalled, not read.
- **Compare screens for easily mixed-up Facts.** `data/groups.json` lists 15 groups, such as the four parking distances. Once every Fact in a group is learned and none of them was answered in the last 12 hours, the next Set opens with the group's Compare screen, the Facts side by side, then one question on each, and takes no new Facts. A group is compared again only after one of its Facts is answered wrong, and again not within 12 hours of that answer. In a group of 4 or more with a reverse stem, every second question is asked the other way round ("You may NOT park within 30 feet of which of these?"), with no sign picture, since the picture would give the answer away. A Set with a group block and too few other learned Facts to fill it can have fewer than 5 counted questions. A reverse question never offers a second true choice: a-172 and a-176 are not reversed, since each one's answer is also true of the other's label (#41).
- **Other wordings.** A fact is asked in each of its wordings in turn (`fact` in `data/questions.json`): a counted question uses the wording with the fewest counted answers, the app's own question first on a tie, so the Learner learns the rule and not one question's words, as the real test words things its own way. First looks and Practice tests use the app's own question. Every fact in a Compare group has at least two wordings (#36).
- **The result comes first.** After any answer, and on the screen after a first look, the first line of the card is the result: "You picked the right answer: X." in green, or "The answer is: X." with "You picked: Y." under it. Every other line on the Learner's screens is a full sentence, and the answer is always called "the answer" (#55).
- **No manual quotes on the Learner's screens.** Each question keeps its verbatim manual quote and page in `data/questions.json`, which is how it is checked against the manual, but the app shows only the answer and its plain explanation. The Helper's view still gives the page for a question missed often.
- **Every question is multiple choice**, as on the real knowledge test. Typed-number answers were built (#46) and removed the same day: the app practices the test as it is, not a harder or different one.
- **A wrong counted answer comes back twice:** once more in the same Set, after 3 question screens for other Facts (at most 4 re-asks a Set), and again in the next Set. A re-ask never changes a Standing. When a wrong choice is another Fact's right answer, the feedback names the question it answers.
- **Wording is literal.** After a wrong answer the page gives the right answer and says where the question comes back, never "Not correct." Set done gives counts of Facts learned and coming back, no score; a Practice test shows how many were right, and below the ready bar says what it is short of, never that it would pass or fail the real test (#35). No idioms ("Not quite", "stay sharp"), no gesture words like "Tap", and the page says it is a practice app not made by the State of Tennessee.
- **Practice tests unlock** once every Fact is learned, or when a Helper's Move unlocks them. Their wrong answers make those Facts due in the next Set, and their right answers count toward spacing. The home screen lists the last five scores.
- **"Ready for the test"** means 27 or more of 30 on three practice tests in a row (90%). The real test passes at 24 of 30 (80%); the extra margin allows for test-day nerves.
- **Order.** New Facts come in the order of `data/sections.json`, so each Section's run easiest first. Due and other Facts are shuffled among them, and answer choices are shuffled every time, re-asks included.
- **Hidden links `#restart` and `#section<N>` were removed.** On an Instance they would change only one device's copy. Reset progress and Move, under "Manage this Learner" in the Helper's view, replace them.
- **Nothing on the Learner's pages is addressed to the Helper.** Every word there is addressed to the Learner. The Helper's view speaks to the Helper in the same plain, literal style. Instructions for running an Instance belong outside the app (`docs/self-hosting.md`).
