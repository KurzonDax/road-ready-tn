# Surface brief: the Learner's screens (src/app.html)

Scope: every Learner screen in the single page (sign-in, home, Section questions, practice quiz and practice test screens, the finished screens), with the Helper's view inheriting the tokens. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: one adult autistic Learner studying alone on a phone or laptop, usually at night. One thing to do on each screen, plain literal words, calm feedback. Copy, flow and engine are unchanged; this is a visual replacement.

Chosen direction: Night route (assigned by the roll, picked by Randy on 2026-10-20 from three mockups in .impeccable/mocks/redesign/). Memorable moment: the home map, where the 120 questions are a route from Start to Practice tests and your place on it is the navigation puck.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is turn-by-turn navigation driven at night: one maneuver banner says the one thing to do, a dark map shows how far you have come, a sheet holds the rest. It refuses the stacked equal-weight blocks of a form page and the gamified quiz card.

OWN-WORLD: Night map ground #0d1317 with a faint street grid; sheet panels #172027 with hairline inner edges; the maneuver banner in deep route blue #1a58c2 with a white action button; route blue #4c98ff marks you (your car's outline, your path drawn as a cased route line, your puck). Green #5fd08a only for correct, coral #f27c6b only for incorrect. One family, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, 400 to 800. Large radii (16 to 28px).

STORY: The Learner sees one banner and knows what to do, sees the route and knows how far along he is, acts on a drawing in which his car and path are route blue, and reads the result first.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the blue banner at the top with the heading (Practice quiz, Section N, Practice tests) at 40px weight 800 and its white full-width button; under it, attached, a dark card for what comes next with a quiet button; then the night map (full-bleed on phones) with the route, eleven Section marks, Start and Practice tests pins and the puck; then a sheet with Questions learned N of 120, the Sections panel and the fine print.

FORM: Turn-by-turn navigation in night mode, position 4 of the grounded list (nav night mode, highway signage, instrument cluster, nav, state map, owner's manual drawings, workbook), seed key 30dbeedd. Raise from the declined emission-line challenger: state by line form as well as hue (solid learned, broken ahead, hatched current).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
